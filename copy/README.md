## English (`en`)

- **Positioning:** An open-source organization making AI useful to everyone.
- **Gloss of the motto:** Through hardship, to the stars.
- **Who we are:** AI for everyone, not just the few. — CatVinci Studio is an open-source organization. We build free tools that bring AI into the everyday work of ordinary people — writing, reading, organising — with nothing new to learn and nothing to pay for.
- **Philosophy:** AI should be within everyone's reach.
  - **Free and open source** — Every line of code is public, and anyone can use, check and improve it, free of charge.
  - **No barriers** — Nothing new to learn and no technical know-how needed: open it and start.
  - **Made for everyone** — For students, writers and anyone who works with words, in ten languages and counting.
  - **Privacy first** — Your files stay on your own computer, and you decide which AI to use and what it sees.
- **Join us:** Make the journey with us. — Whether you love code, writing or art, you are welcome here. Leave your address and a line about yourself, and we will write back.
