## Highlights · 亮点

- **Writing · 写作** — every Markdown mark styled as you type; formulas,
  tables and diagrams; zen, typewriter, split and source modes; themes,
  including Typora themes; export to PDF, Word, HTML and more. ·
  每一种 Markdown 标记边写边排好；公式、表格、图表；禅模式、打字机、分屏、
  源码四种写作模式；主题一键更换，Typora 主题也能直接用；导出为 PDF、Word、
  HTML 等格式。
- **Textus AI** — grey continuations you accept with Tab; spelling and
  grammar checks against your own writing rules; edits proposed with
  reasons, taken whole or sentence by sentence; notes pinned beside the
  text. · 停笔即续写，按 Tab 收下；对照你的写作规范检查错字和语病；修改以提议
  呈现并附理由，可整段采纳或逐句取舍；批注钉在原文旁。
- **Knows you · 懂你** — learns your style, remembers your characters and
  places, follows your rules. · 学习你的文风，记住你笔下的人物与地点，遵循你
  定下的写作要求。
- **Your choice of AI · LLM 随你选** — 20+ providers, or any
  OpenAI-compatible service. · 支持 20 多种 LLM，以及任何兼容 OpenAI 接口的
  服务。
- **Private · 隐私** — your files stay on your computer. · 文件留在你自己的电脑上。
