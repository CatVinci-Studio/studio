## 中文介绍

### 一句话（≤ 30 字）
Textus：免费开源的 Markdown 编辑器，AI 只提建议，笔在你手里。

### 短版（约 60 字）

### 中版（约 150 字）
Textus 是 CatVinci Studio 开发的下一代 Markdown 编辑器，支持 macOS、Windows 和 Linux，免费并以 MIT 协议开源。

Markdown 边写边渲染，公式、表格、Mermaid 图表都能直接编辑，文件就是普通的 .md。内置的 AI 写作助手不会替你写：停笔时灰字续写，按 Tab 采纳；改稿附带理由，可以一句一句地收；批注贴在页边，不动正文。可接入 DeepSeek、通义千问、OpenAI、Claude 等 20 多家模型，或用 Ollama 在本地运行。无需注册，没有遥测。

### 长版（约 400 字）
写作的人都知道，最难的不是排版，而是保持思路不断。Textus 想做的，就是一个让人专心写字的 Markdown 编辑器，再配一位懂分寸的 AI 助手。

**写作**：Markdown 标记边写边渲染，指向某一行就显示它的源码。公式、表格、脚注、Mermaid 图表都能直接编辑。专注、打字机、分屏、源码四种模式随时切换，界面风格多样，Typora 主题可以直接用。写完可以导出为 PDF、Word、HTML 等格式。

**Textus AI**：停笔时，后面的字以灰色出现，按 Tab 采纳；错别字和语法问题边写边标出，Alt+Enter 修正。需要改稿时，它给出的是带理由的建议，可以整段接受，也可以一句一句地挑。批注钉在对应的行旁边，是笔记，不是重写。它会学习你的文风，记住你笔下的人物和地点。

**任选模型**：支持 20 多家大模型和任何 OpenAI 兼容接口，可以直接登录 ChatGPT 或 Claude，也可以用 Ollama 在本地运行。

**隐私**：文档是普通的 Markdown 文件，存在你选择的位置；API Key 只保存在本机；AI 请求从你的设备直接发往你选择的服务商。无需注册，没有统计、遥测和广告。代码全部公开，任何人都可以查验。

Textus 免费、MIT 开源，支持 macOS、Windows 和 Linux，iPhone 和 iPad 版即将推出。

我们一直在用 Markdown 写东西，也一直在用 AI。但大多数 AI 写作工具的思路是“让 AI 替你写”，结果文字越来越不像自己。Textus 反过来：笔始终在你手里，AI 只在旁边提建议、做批注，就像一位细心的编辑。我们把它做成免费开源的，因为我们相信好用的 AI 工具应该属于每个人。

## English

### One-liner (≤ 15 words)
Textus is a free, open-source Markdown editor where the AI suggests and you decide.

### Short (~40 words)
Textus is a free, open-source WYSIWYG Markdown editor for macOS, Windows and Linux. Its optional AI writing assistant completes, proofreads and revises, but every change arrives as a suggestion you can accept or ignore.

### Medium (~120 words)
Textus is the next-generation Markdown editor from CatVinci Studio. It's free and MIT-licensed, and runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.

Markdown renders as you type. Math, tables and Mermaid diagrams are supported, and your files stay plain .md. The built-in AI doesn't write for you. When you pause, a completion appears in grey and Tab accepts it. Edits arrive as proposals with a reason, and you can accept them sentence by sentence. Comments are pinned in the margin instead of rewriting your text. Connect DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or 20+ other providers, or run a local model with Ollama. There's no account and no telemetry.

### Long (~300 words)
The hard part of writing isn't formatting. It's keeping your train of thought. Textus is a Markdown editor built for focus, with an AI assistant that knows its place.

**Writing.** Every Markdown mark renders as you type, and pointing at a line shows its source. Math, tables, footnotes and Mermaid diagrams are supported. Switch between Zen, Typewriter, Split and Source modes, choose from many interface styles, or bring your Typora themes. Export to PDF, Word, HTML and more.

**Textus AI.** Pause, and the next words appear in grey; Tab takes them. Typos and grammar are flagged as you write, and Alt+Enter fixes them. Ask for an edit and you get proposals with a reason. Take one whole, or go sentence by sentence. Comments are pinned to the lines they're about: notes, not rewrites. It learns your style and remembers your characters and places.

**Any model.** Textus works with more than 20 providers and any OpenAI-compatible API. Sign in with ChatGPT or Claude, add an API key, or run a local model with Ollama.

**Private by design.** Your documents are plain Markdown files, kept wherever you choose. API keys stay on your computer, and AI requests go straight from your device to the provider you pick. There's nothing to sign up for, and no analytics, telemetry or ads. The code is public, so anyone can check.

Textus is free and MIT-licensed for macOS, Windows and Linux, with iPhone and iPad coming soon.
