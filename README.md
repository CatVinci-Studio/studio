# CatVinci Studio

**An open-source organization making AI useful to everyone.**
一个开源组织，让 AI 为每个人所用。

*Per aspera ad astra* — through hardship, to the stars. · 循此苦旅，以达天际。

This repository holds the studio's own materials: its brand, the words it
uses to describe itself, and where to find everything it makes.

## Who we are

AI for everyone, not just the few. We build free tools that bring AI into
the everyday work of ordinary people — writing, reading, organising — with
nothing new to learn and nothing to pay for.

## Philosophy

- **Free and open source** — every line of code is public; anyone can use,
  check and improve it, free of charge.
- **No barriers** — nothing new to learn, no technical know-how needed.
- **Made for everyone** — for students, writers and anyone who works with
  words, in ten languages and counting.
- **Privacy first** — your files stay on your own computer; you decide which
  AI to use and what it sees.
